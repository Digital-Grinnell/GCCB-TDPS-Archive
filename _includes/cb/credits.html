Technical Credits

This digital collection is built with CollectionBuilder, an open source framework for creating digital collection and exhibit websites that is developed by faculty librarians at the University of Idaho Library following the Lib-Static methodology. Using the CollectionBuilder-CSV template and the static website generator Jekyll, this project creates an engaging interface to explore driven by metadata.

The Department of Theatre, Dance, and Performance Studies thanks Chloe Kelly '26, who built this archive through a partnership with the Vivero Digital Fellows Program, a training and mentorship program hosted by the Digital Liberal Arts Collective at Grinnell College.
